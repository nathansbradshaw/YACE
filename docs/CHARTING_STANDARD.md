# Review a draft

Work on one instrument and a short passage at a time. Listen to the original,
then compare the matching stem where available.

Check:

- **Timing:** attacks and releases follow the recording.
- **Coverage:** important notes are present without extra notes from other parts.
- **Patterns:** repeated phrases use consistent lanes and shapes.
- **Playability:** chords, fills and transitions make sense on the controller.
- **Difficulty:** lower tiers keep the musical shape while reducing demand.
- **Vocals:** pitches, words and phrases follow the performance; gaps stay visible.

Replay after editing. Try the exported chart in the target game and on the
controller you intend to support. A successful export or an empty flag queue
does not replace listening and playtesting.

Review flags are prompts. Approve a section after checking it, hide a flag when
you do not want to see it, and record a reason for accepting or dismissing an
issue. See [the editor guide](EDITOR.md).
