# Practice playback

- **Play / Pause** controls the recording. Space works outside text fields.
- **−2 s / +2 s** moves two seconds through the original recording.
- **Follow** keeps the timeline near the playhead.
- **Speed** offers 50%, 75% and 100% with pitch preserved.

Changing speed pauses playback while the audio is prepared. Press Play when it
is ready. The chart and original recording are not stretched or rewritten.

## Loop a passage

Select a review section and choose **Loop section**, or expand **Edit loop**.
Set **In** and **Out**, then choose **Apply loop**. **In here** and **Out here**
use the current playhead position. **Clear loop** removes the shared loop.

Loop positions use original-recording seconds. At half speed, two seconds of
the source take four seconds to play. Clean-audio audition uses the same loop.

The preview is an editing and listening aid. It does not score controller input.
